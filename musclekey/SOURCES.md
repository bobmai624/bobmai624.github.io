## Evidence labels used in the page

- **Official requirement**: a hardware or safety action stated by SparkFun or Advancer Technologies.
- **Measurement standard**: sEMG placement or reporting guidance from SENIAM or ISEK.
- **Research ethics**: current Australian national guidance; the institution decides the applicable review pathway.
- **Project parameter**: a transparent engineering choice made for this prototype, not a manufacturer or scientific standard.
