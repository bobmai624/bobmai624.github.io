# PedalBalance Echo Nine-Reference Model Presentation

## Purpose

Rebuild the interactive PedalBalance model as a nine-scene academic demonstration that is immediately understandable without prior EMS, biomechanics, or HCI knowledge. The nine scenes synthesize the presentation methods of Proprioceptive Interaction, HERMITS, Muscle-Propelled Force Feedback, SwarmHaptics, Wireality, PantoGuide, Linked-Stick, Breaking It Down Is Better, and Learning to Perform a New Movement with Robotic Assistance.

## Narrative

The scenes form one causal story rather than nine source summaries:

1. **Closed loop:** foot input → machine interpretation → ankle cue → active correction.
2. **Modules:** sensors, complete-revolution logic, and bounded vibration output.
3. **Three-frame cause:** normal pedalling → persistent imbalance → voluntary correction after a cue.
4. **Design space:** cue timing, dose, fading, and source are explicit experimental variables.
5. **Measured system:** mechanism constraints and measurable prototype facts sit beside the device.
6. **Participant task:** baseline, cue, no-cue, and replay conditions are stated as actions.
7. **Trace provenance:** past self, matched other, visual replay, and no history remain distinguishable.
8. **Study timeline:** baseline → training → no cue → retention → transfer.
9. **Evidence boundary:** assisted performance is separated from unaided learning evidence.

Each scene names the borrowed presentation method and links to its primary academic source, while all visual content depicts PedalBalance rather than copying the source artwork.

## Interaction and visual system

- Retain the full-screen modal, fixed language controls, keyboard focus trap, Escape close, and global STOP OUTPUT.
- Replace the repeated four-card diagram with one large scene-specific visual built from typography, lines, bars, and CSS shapes.
- Keep headings concise and use one dominant visual behavior per scene.
- Use the existing warm neutral, blue, coral, green, and black palette.
- Treat the nine-scene rail as a progress narrative; mobile uses a horizontally scrollable rail.
- All source links open in a new tab and are labelled as presentation references, not evidence that PedalBalance itself has been validated.

## Language and evidence boundaries

- Chinese, English, and Japanese localize every scene title, summary, participant action, system action, observation, visual label, and reference-method label.
- The model remains an illustrative simulation and planned study.
- Pressure remains a relative complete-revolution proxy, not power, torque, muscle strength, or diagnosis.
- The body output remains a short vibration cue; no DIY EMS/TENS/FES implementation is shown.
- Learning claims require no-cue, retention, and transfer evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- The modal contains exactly nine scenes in the narrative order above.
- Each of the nine primary references contributes a distinct, visible presentation method.
- The first scene explains the entire experiment in one sentence and one closed-loop diagram.
- Scene-specific visuals are readable without their explanatory paragraph.
- Source links are present, HTTPS, and mapped to the correct reference.
- The cue scene visibly differentiates machine intervention from voluntary correction.
- The final scene visually separates assisted performance from unaided learning.
- Existing safety, localization, focus, STOP, download, and portfolio-return behavior remains intact.
- Automated tests and desktop/mobile browser checks pass before publishing.
