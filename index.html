04 / About

Curious in method.
Clear in outcome.

I am a Bachelor of Design student at the University of Melbourne, specialising in User Experience Design. I move between research, visual judgement and structured analysis, choosing the form that makes each problem easier to understand.

My work ranges from interface prototypes and usability studies to publications, spatial narratives and financial models. Across them, the common task is the same: make complex evidence legible enough to support a decision.
